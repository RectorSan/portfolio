About me

One engineer.
Many disciplines.

I started out in Mechatronics Engineering at the Federal University of Technology, Minna, in 2019. There I learned Solidity and worked as a community manager for several Web3 communities. In 2022 I moved to Computer Science at Summit University, Offa, and graduated in 2025 with Second Class Upper honours.

Since 2022 I've worked on PTDF-funded pipeline leak-detection research using CNNs on drone imagery, which is where I picked up grant writing and learned to fly drones. In 2023 I started in UI/UX with a focus on UX research. Today I build cloud infrastructure with Terraform, Ansible, Kubernetes and Argo CD, and full-stack products with TypeScript, React, Node.js and PostgreSQL.

I learn best by building the whole thing, from the infrastructure underneath to the interface on top. I also co-lead the SUNO Tech Community, running workshops and build sessions for students.
