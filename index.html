About me

I love games.
Not just playing them.

I've run multiple gaming communities, and tried building a few of my own. I've lived inside Web3-based games, 3D life-simulation games — every version of The Sims, Supernatural, Career and the rest — and text-based games like Torn City and BitLife. I want to bring the ideas that come out of all that time straight into Lagos Life.

On the community side: I managed Synex Coin, a Minecraft-linked crypto community, for 14 months — moderating, handling player issues, keeping people engaged day to day. I've managed Web3 communities since 2019, and I co-lead the SUNO Tech Community, running workshops for students.

On the building side: Celebrity Games is a multiplayer life-simulation game I built from scratch — Node.js, PostgreSQL, WebSockets, live server events, an in-game economy, interactive career paths. I also worked with Wizzwoods, a Web3 game, representing the team at the Web3 Global Conference.

I studied Computer Science at Summit University, Offa (2022–2025, Second Class Upper), after Mechatronics Engineering at FUT Minna. Alongside community and game work, I build cloud infrastructure, do UX research, and have shipped applied-AI and Web3 projects.
